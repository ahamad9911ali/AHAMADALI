SIGNAL METHODOLOGY

Reading the dashboard

PCR is total put OI divided by total call OI. Change-OI PCR uses put change in OI divided by call change in OI, which surfaces fresh positioning.

The engine scores PCR, call/put OI addition or unwinding, change in OI, price versus VWAP, volume, 3-minute momentum, 5-minute trend, and sector sentiment. No individual factor can create a trade signal.
