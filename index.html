SIGNAL METHODOLOGY

Reading the dashboard

PCR is put open interest divided by call open interest. A rising PCR can show stronger put-side participation; a falling PCR can show stronger call-side participation.

OI build-up compares open-interest changes on calls and puts. The dashboard combines PCR direction, net OI build-up, and the selected 3 or 5 minute price trend to label the current bias.

Signals are informational and can change quickly. Confirm with price action, risk limits, and your own strategy before placing any order.
